STAGE 8 CRITICAL ANALYSIS: WHAT IS MISLEADING ABOUT MY HEADLINE NUMBER?
============================================================

1. The Danger of the 97.70% Headline Precision
------------------------------------------------------------
A naive summary of this project might proclaim:
  'The AppleSupport AI Agent achieves 97.70% Auto-Handle Precision!'

Presenting this number without its denominator is deeply misleading.
Here is why:
 - 97.70% precision is measured ONLY on the 174 inquiries (14.63% of the test set) that passed
   all 7 strict deterministic safety gates.
 - It does NOT mean the AI can automatically solve 97.70% of customer support requests.
 - In fact, 85.37% of all incoming customer requests (1,015 / 1,189) are ESCALATED to human agents.

2. The Safety vs. Automation Coverage Tradeoff
------------------------------------------------------------
The system is intentionally conservative by design:
 - Auto-Handle Rate (Deflection) : 14.63% (174 cases)
 - Escalation Rate (Human Queue) : 85.37% (1,015 cases)
 - False Auto-Handle Rate        : 2.30% (4 / 174 auto-handles)
 - Escalation Recall             : 98.24% (98.24% of uncertain/unsafe cases are intercepted)

In customer support, sending an ungrounded, hallucinated, or incorrect automated reply causes
immediate brand damage, customer churn, and escalated support costs. Therefore, a low automation rate (14.63%)
with high precision (97.70%) and minimal false auto-handles (2.30%) is an intentional, defensible tradeoff.

3. Dataset Selection & Resolution Bias
------------------------------------------------------------
 - The retrieval index and ground-truth validation/test sets originate from historical AppleSupport conversations.
 - As documented in Stage 3, resolved Twitter threads exhibit an inherent selection bias (longer dialogues, active user confirmations).
 - Production deployment would encounter novel hardware releases, zero-day iOS bugs, and angry customer disputes
   that lack exact historical analogues, necessitating robust human fallback.

4. Human Agreement Nuance (62.00%, Kappa = 0.2792)
------------------------------------------------------------
 - On the N=50 human review sample, human agreement is 62.00% with Cohen's Kappa = 0.2792.
 - This modest Kappa reflects the system's deliberate conservatism: human reviewers are willing to take
   educated guesses on ambiguous customer queries, whereas the AI strictly escalates when similarity < 0.65
   or intent confidence < 0.60.
