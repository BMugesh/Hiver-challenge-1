1. Objective
------------------------------
Discover a compact, meaningful, defensible customer-support intent taxonomy from
the 7,922 curated historical AppleSupport conversations (`apple_support_resolved_threads.json`).
The taxonomy reflects what the customer needs help with to facilitate similarity retrieval
and classification without target label leakage.

------------------------------
 - Primary Input: data/processed/apple_support_resolved_threads.json (7,922 resolved cases)
 - Auxiliary Signal: data/raw/apple_support_turn_structure.csv (104,831 agent turns)

6. Candidate Intent Groups Identified
------------------------------
Exploratory text clustering and keyword signatures identified 11 natural problem clusters:
 1. Autocorrect / typing glitches (dominant in iOS 11 launch period)
 2. Battery health, charging, and sudden battery drain
 3. WiFi, Bluetooth, Cellular, and network connection dropouts
 4. Touchscreen unresponsiveness, screen freeze, and display brightness
 5. Apple ID access, password recovery, 2FA, and iCloud storage
 6. App Store purchases, subscriptions, billing, and refunds
 7. App crashes, freezing on open, and App Store download errors
 8. Audio, microphone, speaker, and AirPods connectivity
 9. Operating system updates, restore via iTunes, and lag
 10. How-to feature guidance, settings navigation, and control center
 11. General device inquiries and hardware diagnostics

7. Taxonomy Design Principles
------------------------------
 - Customer-Centric: Represents 'what the customer needs help with', not agent actions.
 - Retrieval Utility: Groups problems where AppleSupport provides similar troubleshooting steps.
 - Balanced Granularity: Avoids monolithic catch-alls (e.g. TECHNICAL_ISSUE) and hyper-fragmentation.
 - Sufficient Support: Every intent has at least 190+ verified historical cases.

8. Merge / Split Decisions Summary
------------------------------
 - SPLIT: GENERAL_TECHNICAL_ISSUE was partitioned by text symptoms into KEYBOARD_TYPING_AUTOCORRECT,
   AUDIO_SOUND_SPEAKER, and specific hardware/software intents.
 - MERGED: MEDIA_MUSIC_STORE and BILLING_PAYMENT_SUBSCRIPTION were merged into APP_STORE_PURCHASES_BILLING
   because customer inquiries and support workflows (refunds/subscriptions) are identical.
 - MERGED: AMBIGUOUS_INQUIRY was merged into GENERAL_DEVICE_INQUIRY due to near-zero frequency (2 cases).

11. Final Intent Taxonomy (11 Intents)
------------------------------
[KEYBOARD_TYPING_AUTOCORRECT] (585 cases, 7.38%)
  Description: Keyboard unresponsiveness, predictive text malfunctions, and autocorrect glyph glitches (including the iOS 11 letter 'I' substitution bug).

[BATTERY_CHARGING_POWER] (743 cases, 9.38%)

[DISPLAY_TOUCH_SCREEN] (654 cases, 8.26%)
  Description: Touchscreen unresponsiveness, display freezing, screen flickering, brightness adjustments, or biometric authentication (Face ID / Touch ID).

[ACCOUNT_APPLEID_ICLOUD] (401 cases, 5.06%)

[APP_STORE_PURCHASES_BILLING] (558 cases, 7.04%)
  Description: App Store purchases, in-app billing, Apple Music subscriptions, duplicate charges, payment methods, and refund requests.

[APP_CRASH_AND_DOWNLOAD] (192 cases, 2.42%)
  Description: First-party and third-party applications freezing, crashing on launch, or failing to download/install from the App Store.

[AUDIO_SOUND_SPEAKER] (328 cases, 4.14%)
  Description: Speaker distortion, microphone input failure, call audio issues, volume controls, or AirPods audio disconnects.

[OS_UPDATE_SYSTEM_PERFORMANCE] (1,403 cases, 17.71%)
  Description: General system performance degradation, software update installation errors, device restore, and operating system bugs.

[HOW_TO_SETTINGS_CONFIGURATION] (212 cases, 2.68%)
  Description: Questions on how to locate, configure, or customize specific iOS features, control center items, and device settings.

[GENERAL_DEVICE_INQUIRY] (2,259 cases, 28.52%)
  Description: Miscellaneous device inquiries, general hardware questions, or complex multi-symptom inquiries.

16. Taxonomy Justification & Empirical Disclosure
------------------------------
Why did we choose this particular intent taxonomy?
 1. Grounded in Actual AppleSupport Data: Derived from real customer inquiry phrasing and symptoms.
 2. Action Similarity: Groups inquiries that share identical troubleshooting steps or resolution workflows.
 3. Class Support: Every intent has at least 190+ verified historical cases (min 2.42%, max 28.52%).
 4. Retrieval Precision: Provides optimal granularity for indexing without fragmented sparse clusters.
 5. Classification Reliability: High inter-class distinction based on observable technical symptoms.

Important Trust & Non-Overclaim Statement:
 Intent discovery is not the same as discovering the 'true' universal set of customer intents.
 This taxonomy is a practical engineering abstraction derived specifically for this AppleSupport dataset.
 Different reasonable taxonomies may exist. Downstream evaluation in Stage 8 will empirically
 benchmark classification accuracy and retrieval relevance using this taxonomy.

17. Final Recommendation for Stage 5 (Retrieval & RAG)
------------------------------
 Use the 11-intent taxonomy to index and filter the 7,922 resolved historical cases in `apple_support_resolved_threads.json`.
 Use `train.csv` (5,545 cases) for candidate indexing and classifier training, and evaluate retrieval precision on `test.csv` (1,189 cases).
