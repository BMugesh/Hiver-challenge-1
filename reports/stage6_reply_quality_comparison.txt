================================================================================
1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & ARCHITECTURAL UPGRADE
================================================================================

Prior to this upgrade, the Stage 6 generation layer suffered from a critical
disconnect: while Stage 4 intent classification (80.24%) and Stage 5 domain-aware
retrieval (71.24% Recall@1, 0.7767 MRR) successfully surfaced strong historical
evidence, the generation module treated raw historical Twitter text as direct output
sources. Because historical multi-turn Twitter dialogues frequently began with
conversational triage questions ("What iOS version are you on?"), the generated reply
defaulted to generic clarification questions—ignoring the concrete resolution pattern.

Under the new architecture:
1. AI Proposes -> Domain-Aware Intent & Candidate Historical Cases.
2. Evidence Constrains -> Structured Resolution Patterns & Domain Facts extracted as first-class generation inputs.
3. Explicit Mode Priority -> Enforces `EVIDENCE_BACKED_ACTION` when intent confidence and similarity exceed thresholds.
4. Verifier Audits -> IndependentGroundingVerifier audits Resolution Faithfulness (PASS/FAIL/NOT_APPLICABLE).
5. Safety Policy Decides -> AUTO-HANDLE vs. ESCALATE based on confidence, similarity, and verifier audit.
